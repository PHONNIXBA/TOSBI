TOSBI Risk protection infrastructure for perpetual DEXs

Protection for the position a trader already has.

TOSBI lets any perpetual DEX sell one-tap downside protection on open positions. The DEX supplies the capital and earns the premiums. We supply the contracts, pricing, settlement, and risk controls, and earn a fee on every policy. Launching on Stellar.

The business in one sentence

We are a B2B2C infrastructure company. We do not sell to traders and we do not hold risk. We license a protection layer to perpetual DEXs, they put it in front of their traders, and every policy sold pays us a fee.

Why this exists

Perpetual futures are the largest product in crypto. The trader's central problem is liquidation: a leveraged position that moves 10% the wrong way is gone. The tools for managing that risk are a stop-loss, which throws the trader out of a position they wanted to keep, or an options hedge on a separate venue, which almost no retail trader knows how to build.

$60T+perpetual futures volume in 2025, mostly offshore and increasingly on-chain
$10Bliquidated on a single venue in one October 2025 crash day
0position-attached protection products on any on-chain perp DEX today

Bybit proved the demand. Its Perp Protect attaches an option to a perpetual position behind one button, and it has become the product traders point to when they ask for "insurance." On-chain, nothing equivalent exists. The perp DEXs that now process hundreds of billions a month have liquidation engines and insurance funds that protect the venue. None of them protects the trader.

That is the gap: a proven product, a market that has moved on-chain, and no one supplying it there.

How the product works

From the trader's side it is three choices, one price, and one automatic outcome. Every contract has a fixed start, a fixed end, and a fixed maximum, all set the moment it is bought.

A short position gets the mirror image: protection covers the first 25% of a rise, from $0.20 to $0.25, capped at $250. Rises are usually gentler than crashes, so short protection is priced from a separate volatility figure and costs about $25 on the same terms.

How the money flows

There is one pool of capital, owned by the PerpDEX, and one rule: the pool can never promise more than it holds. Every policy reserves its maximum payout for its whole life. If a new policy would break the rule, it isn't sold.

Why full backing, and not a fraction of it. A real insurer holds a fraction of its liabilities because claims are uncorrelated. Crypto crashes are the opposite: every protected long claims on the same day. A pool holding 40% of its promises goes bankrupt in exactly the event it was sold for. Full backing costs capital efficiency and buys a guarantee we can put in a contract.

How we protect the pool

Full backing makes insolvency impossible. These limits decide how much of the pool any single event can consume, and they are enforced by the contract, not by policy.

ThreatControl
One asset crashes and everyone protecting it claims at once25% cap per asset-and-direction; 40% per asset
A crowd piles into the same protection before a known eventPremium rises automatically with concentration; surcharge around scheduled events
A trader moves the price to trigger a payoutSettlement on a 20–30 minute multi-source average; policies sized to the cost of moving the market
Buy protection, close the position, keep the optionProtection is bound to the position and settles the moment the position closes
One whale drains the pool2% of pool per wallet
Volatility doubles in an hourShort-duration sales pause 15 minutes while prices refresh

The single number we manage to is the loss ratio: payouts divided by premiums. Target below 80% on a rolling month. Above 100% for two weeks raises prices; well below 40% for two months lowers them.

Go-to-market

We are starting on Stellar on purpose. It has five-second finality, negligible fees, native USDC, a production oracle, and a first wave of perpetual DEXs that are early enough to co-design with. There is no options venue on the chain for a DEX to route to instead of us.

  1. Phase 1: Stellar, one launch partnerIntegrate with one perp DEX at testnet, then mainnet. Prove the loss ratio over a full volatility cycle. Fund the build through the Stellar Community Fund, which has already backed oracle, perp, options, and insurance projects on the chain.
  2. Phase 1b: every Stellar perp DEXThe integration is a position-data interface and a button. Once one DEX runs it, the next takes weeks.
  3. Phase 2: tranched capitalLet outside capital supply a senior layer above the PerpDEX's first-loss slice. This is how the pool grows past one venue's balance sheet, and where our capital-management fee begins.
  4. Phase 3: other chainsThe contracts are chain-specific; the pricing engine, risk controls, and partner playbook are not. Target the chains where perp DEXs are largest and no protection layer exists.
What a launch partner commits. An initial capital deposit sized to about two weeks of expected demand, on-chain position data including close prices, a "Protect" button in the position view, and a joint process for changing risk parameters.

What could go wrong

RiskWhat we do about it
Pricing is wrong and the pool loses moneyThe loss ratio is measured weekly and prices move on a rule, not a meeting. Full backing means a mispriced month is a bad month, not a failure.
Stellar perp DEXs stay smallThe integration cost is low enough to run on every one of them; the pricing engine is chain-agnostic and Phase 3 does not depend on Stellar volume.
The oracle fails or is attackedMulti-source averaging, staleness checks, per-update caps, and a pause. Settlement waits for a valid price rather than using a bad one.
A DEX builds its ownThey would need the pricing, the risk controls, and the capital rules, and they would carry the underwriting on a single venue's book. Our fee is small relative to that build and that risk.
Capital is inefficient at 100% backingTrue, and accepted for v1. Tranching in v2 brings in cheaper capital; hedging is deferred until a deep on-chain perp venue exists to hedge on.
